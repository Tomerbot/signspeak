    About SignSpeak

    SignSpeak helps hearing people take their first steps in American Sign Language (ASL), so it's a little easier to say hello to a Deaf classmate, neighbor or family member.

    How it works

    Google's MediaPipe AI finds 21 points on your hand and 6 points on your face in every camera frame. SignSpeak then checks the three parts that make up a sign: the handshape, the place (compared with your face) and the movement over the last second.

    Private

    Everything runs inside your browser. The video is never recorded or uploaded, and there is no account.

    What it can't do

    SignSpeak knows 10 one-handed signs. Real ASL is a full language with its own grammar, facial expressions and two-handed signs, and it's different from other sign languages (Israeli Sign Language has different signs). SignSpeak is a first step, not a translator. To really learn ASL, learn from Deaf teachers, for example at Lifeprint (ASL University).
