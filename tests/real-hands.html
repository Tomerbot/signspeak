Real hands check

Runs MediaPipe on photos of ASL alphabet letters (dataset: Marxulia/asl_sign_languages_alphabets_v03 on Hugging Face) and checks that SignSpeak's handshape rules agree. Several letters use the same handshapes as SignSpeak's signs: A, S and E are closed hands (a fist), B is a flat hand, O is close to a flat O, U and V are two fingers. Y, L, W, I and C must not be mistaken for any of SignSpeak's handshapes.
